Sequence utility

DNA Sequence Analyzer

Paste raw DNA, multiline DNA, or one FASTA record. A, T, G, C, and optional N bases are supported.

Quick educational analysis

Whitespace is removed. If a FASTA header is present, the first line beginning with “>” is treated as the header.

Your cleaned sequence, composition, GC/AT percentages, and reverse complement will appear here.

Primer workflow

Primer Design & Quick Check

Check one primer or compare a forward/reverse pair using simple sequence composition and the Wallace Tm approximation.

Not a specificity test

Only A, T, G, and C are accepted here. Approximate Tm uses 2 × (A + T) + 4 × (G + C).

Enter a forward primer, and optionally a reverse primer, to see quick composition and approximate Tm results.

About BioResource Finder

BioResource Finder helps biology students and researchers move from a biological question to an appropriate next step, quick calculation, learning resource, or professional scientific tool.

Scientific limitation: Built-in calculations are educational utilities and should not replace validated experimental protocols, professional analysis software, or official scientific documentation.
